## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: static HTML/CSS/vanilla JS, no build step, so it deploys as-is on GitHub Pages (the user's chosen host for now; HostGator possible later). No CMS; content updates are done by editing files.

## Users

1. **Agencies that outsource design** (white label), mainly São Paulo region, looking for a reliable social media / branding designer who delivers fast and organized.
2. **Businesses and professionals who want a monthly social media subscription** (feed + stories + identity) — clinics, architects, personal brands, music scene.

Ideal client: arrives knowing what they want and gives creative freedom. Visitors usually land from a WhatsApp/Instagram link (bio link pointing straight to a case), on a phone.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Alan Veloso that turns visitors into WhatsApp conversations. Success: at least 3 extra clients per month. Prices are shown on a dedicated Planos page (added 2026-11-09 at the user's request), using the exact values of the commercial proposal; custom scope and agency partnerships go to "orçamento sob medida".

## Positioning

Social media designer with branding: "Design para redes sociais com identidade, impacto e consistência visual e branding." Range is the differentiator: moves from clinical/clean (dental, architecture) to authorial/urban (grime, bass scene) while keeping a strong identity per client.

## Operating Context

Process: arrives at the briefing meeting already with references and a sketch → develops the project → presents the visual proposal → aligns adjustments → delivers. Works Monday–Friday, 9h–22h. Deliveries in a few business days.
